FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a prescription?

Medicines marked Rx need a valid doctor’s prescription. You upload it at checkout, and over-the-counter items don’t need one.

How fast is delivery?

Enter your pincode in the bag to see the delivery time and fee for your area. Orders above ₹1,500 get free delivery.

Can I pay online?

This store is a demo, so payments aren’t switched on yet and no order is actually delivered.

Are the stock numbers live?

Stock updates as orders are placed and as the pharmacist restocks. Low-stock items show an amber badge.
